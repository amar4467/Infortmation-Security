01 / THE IDEA

ChaCha20 is a stream cipher. It creates a pseudorandom keystream, then combines that stream with your message using XOR. The same keystream reverses the process.

NO MAGIC. JUST MATHEMATICS.

03 / UNDER THE HOOD

Small operations.
Big transformation.

ChaCha20 builds a 512-bit state from constants, a 256-bit key, a counter and a 96-bit nonce. Its quarter-round mixes four 32-bit words using addition, XOR and rotation.
